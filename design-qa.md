## Reference and scope

The user supplied the transparent PK Hockey Academy crest and the one-slide training flyer, and requested the flyer as a general branding direction. The original crest is preserved in `web/src/assets/brand/pk-hockey-crest.png` (1254 × 1254). Its SHA-256 and provenance are recorded in `ASSETS.md`. The flyer is a flattened 1024 × 1536 image, extracted locally to `output/design/reference/flyer.png` for comparison. It is not published. The registration PDF is outside this task.

This is a responsive interpretation of a print flyer, so print dimensions and individual panels are not copied literally. The matching features are the supplied crest, ice-white surfaces, deep navy condensed headings, cobalt emphasis, “Skills. Mindset. Zukunft.” and the prominent On-Ice/Off-Ice training bands. The existing licensed hockey photograph remains visibly labeled as a stock symbol image. No trainer portrait, credentials, phone number or booking destination was inferred from the flattened flyer.

## Findings fixed and verified

- P1: A legacy mobile wordmark selector enlarged the new tagline and caused overflow. Removed the obsolete selector. All ten routes subsequently measured no horizontal overflow at 375, 768 and 1440 pixels.
- P2: The old downward parallax exposed a gap above the new split photo. Changed it to a conservative upward movement and verified the image covered its frame at the top and end of the hero scroll range. Recaptured desktop evidence.
- Focus outline adjusted to remain visible on both light and navy surfaces.

No actionable P0, P1 or P2 findings remain in this scope. A standalone approved Pierre portrait is a future asset improvement; the current crest graphic is explicitly labeled. Registration remains deferred by the user.
